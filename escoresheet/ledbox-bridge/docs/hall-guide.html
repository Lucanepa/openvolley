LedBox — Hall Guide

Setting up the LED scoreboard at a venue. Follow the steps in order. Don't skip the green checks.

⚠ Do this ONCE at home, before the first match

In a hall there is no wifi you trust, so your phone's hotspot is how you reach the scoreboard controller. The Pi has to learn that hotspot once, at home — it cannot learn it at the venue, because you need the connection in order to set it up.

How: turn your phone's hotspot ON, then from a computer on your home network run:
ssh openvolley
sudo nmcli device wifi connect "YOUR-HOTSPOT-NAME" password "YOUR-PASSWORD"
Do this once. From then on the Pi joins your hotspot automatically whenever it sees it.

1 · What to bring

  • LedBox scoreboardthe panel itself
  • LedBox power cablemains plug
  • The Pi — small box labelled OpenVolleythis is the brain; nothing works without it
  • Pi power supplyUSB-C. Use THIS one, not a phone charger
  • Ethernet cableat least 3 m — links Pi to the board
  • Spare ethernet cablethe #1 thing that fails
  • Power strip + extension leadyou will be short of sockets
  • Your phonethis is the remote control

2 · Setting up — in this order

  1. Hang or stand the board where players can see it. Plug it into mains.
    Do the board first — it takes the longest to start.
    ✓ You should see: the board lights up within about 30 seconds and shows a waiting/idle screen.
  2. Plug the ethernet cable from the board into the Pi.
    Either end, either way round — it is not directional.
    ✓ You should see: small lights blinking at both sockets.
  3. Turn ON your phone's hotspot. Leave it on for the whole match.
    This is the private network the Pi and your phone talk over. No hotspot, no control.
  4. Now plug in the Pi. Wait 60 seconds.
    Always AFTER the hotspot is on, so it can join straight away. Do not unplug it while it starts.
    ✓ You should see: your phone's hotspot screen reports 1 device connected.
  5. On your phone, open the browser and go to:
    http://raspberrypi.local:8890
    Bookmark it now, on the day, while it works. If that address fails see Trouble 2.
    ✓ You should see: the control page, with team panels and big + / − buttons.
  6. Look at the dot at the top-left of the page.
    🟢 green = talking to the board, you are ready. 🔴 red = not talking to it — see Trouble 1.
  7. Type the two team names, set the colours, and start scoring.
    Turn the phone sideways (landscape). Press the full-screen button (the square icon, top right) so nothing is in the way.

3 · Using it during the match

ButtonWhat it does
+ and −Adds or removes a point. The serve indicator follows the point automatically.
SETSets won. Normally counts itself when a set ends — only touch it to correct a mistake.
T/OTimeouts used. Pressing + also starts a 30-second countdown on the board.
SUBSubstitutions used by that team.
Serve LEFT / RIGHTForces who is serving. Use after a mistake — normally it is automatic.
⇄ SwapTeams change ends. Swaps names, colours and scores together.
ResetClears everything back to 0–0. Asks first. Use between matches.
BlankEmpties the board but keeps the score in the app. Use during breaks.
Full screen
square icon, top right		Fills the phone screen with the controls. Press again to leave.

Keep in mind

Keep the phone's hotspot ON and the phone awake — if the phone sleeps the hotspot may drop and you lose control of the board. The score is safe: it lives on the Pi, not on the phone. Reconnect and it is all still there.

4 · Packing up

  1. Press Blank in the app.
    Leaves the board empty and tidy.
  2. Unplug the board from mains.
  3. Unplug the Pi.
    It has no on/off switch. Nothing is lost — it starts by itself next time.
    Better, if you have a laptop: run ssh openvolley "sudo poweroff", wait 20 seconds, then unplug. Cleaner for the memory card. Pulling the plug is normally fine — just avoid doing it while it is still starting up.
  4. Turn off your phone's hotspot. Coil the cables. Count them.

5 · Troubleshooting

Work down the list. Nine times out of ten it is the first row.

ProblemWhat it meansWhat to do
Trouble 1 — Red dot 🔴 in the app The Pi is fine, but it cannot reach the board. Check the ethernet cable at BOTH ends — push until it clicks. Check the board is powered and lit. Try the spare cable. It reconnects on its own within about 5 seconds; no need to restart anything.
Trouble 2 — The page will not open Your phone cannot find the Pi by name. First: is the hotspot ON and does it show 1 device connected? If yes, open your hotspot's connected-devices list, read the Pi's address (e.g. 192.168.43.5) and use http://THAT-ADDRESS:8890 instead. Write it on this sheet.
Trouble 3 — Board frozen on an old score The link dropped and came back. Press any + then − in the app. That forces a full repaint of the board.
Trouble 4 — Board stuck on the waiting screen The Pi has not taken control of it yet. Wait 60 seconds from powering the Pi. If still stuck, unplug the Pi, wait 10 seconds, plug it back in.
Trouble 5 — Team name too long / cut off Known limitation. The name field on the board fits only short names. Use a short name — WIE, VOL, GEN. Three or four letters always fit. This is the reliable fix today.
Trouble 6 — Page is blank / buttons missing Stale page in the phone's browser. Pull down to refresh, or close the tab and reopen the address.
Trouble 7 — Nothing works at all Start from zero. Unplug BOTH the board and the Pi. Count to ten. Plug in the board first, wait for it to light. Then plug in the Pi and wait 60 seconds. Reopen the page.
Trouble 8 — Someone must check deeper For whoever has a laptop. ssh openvolley then systemctl status ledbox-bridge to see if it is running, and journalctl -u ledbox-bridge -n 50 to read what went wrong. sudo systemctl restart ledbox-bridge restarts it.
