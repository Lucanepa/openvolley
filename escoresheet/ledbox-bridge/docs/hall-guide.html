LedBox — Hall Guide

Set up the LED scoreboard and run a match. Follow the steps in order; don't skip the green checks.

The one rule

The scoreboard makes its own wifi. The Pi joins it by itself, and your phone joins it too — scan the codes below. No cable to the board, no venue wifi, nothing to set up on the day. Your phone must be on that wifi, not on mobile data.

SCAN 1
Join the scoreboard's wifi
ledbox_C0270 · 47561052
Point your camera at the code → tap "Join". Then turn mobile data OFF.
SCAN 2
Open the controller
http://172.24.1.60:8890
Scan after you've joined the wifi. Bookmark the page when it opens.

2 · Setting up — in this order

  1. Stand or hang the board where players can see it. Plug it into mains.
    Do the board first — it creates the wifi everything else joins.
    ✓ You should see: the board lights up within ~30s and shows a waiting screen.
  2. Plug in the Pi (the OpenVolley box). Wait 60 seconds.
    It finds the board's wifi and connects on its own. Don't unplug it while it starts.
    ✓ You should see: the board switches to HOME 0 – 0 AWAY — the Pi has taken over.
  3. Scan SCAN 1 to join the wifi, then turn mobile data OFF.
    The wifi has no internet — that's normal. Phones jump back to mobile data unless you stop them.
  4. Scan SCAN 2 to open the controller. Bookmark it.
    ✓ You should see: the control page — team panels, big + / − buttons.
  5. Check the dot at the top-left.
    🟢 green = talking to the board, ready. 🔴 red = see Trouble 1.
  6. Type both team names, pick colours. Turn the phone sideways; press ⛶ for full screen.
    Use SHORT names — WIE, VOL. Long names overflow on the board for now.

3 · Running the match — and your live test

Do these in order the first time; it doubles as a full check that everything works.

Do thisOn the board you should see
Tap + on a teamThe point appears and blinks in that team's colour; the serve indicator moves to that side.
Tap − (a correction)The point drops. It does not blink — only real points blink.
T/O + for a teamBoard switches to a timeout screen: "TO <team>", the score, and a 30s clock. At zero, the horn sounds. Press ✕ to end early — no horn.
T/O + again (2nd)That timeout counter turns dark red — none left.
Sub +The sub counter blinks. At 5 it goes amber, at 6 dark red.
Reach 25 (2-point lead)The set closes and is recorded; board shows the INTERVAL screen (sets only). Press "Start next set →" to skip the wait; the score resets and ends swap.
⏱ Warm-upFull-screen WARM UP clock (10 min). Horn at zero.
🏐 VS screenThe two names with VS — a pre-match screen.
⇄ SwapTeams change ends — names, colours, scores swap together.
⚙ SettingsBlink on/off, countdown lengths, horn on/off, best-of, club name. Saved on the Pi.

Keep in mind

Keep the phone on the ledbox_C0270 wifi. Wander out of range and the page stops responding — walk back and it reconnects. The score is safe: it lives on the Pi, not the phone, so you can lock the phone, swap phones, or hand over — nothing is lost.

4 · Packing up

  1. Press Blank in the app.
    Leaves the board empty and tidy.
  2. Unplug the board from mains.
  3. Unplug the Pi.
    No on/off switch. Nothing is lost — it starts itself next time.
    Cleaner, if you have a laptop: ssh openvolley "sudo poweroff", wait 20s, then unplug. Pulling the plug is normally fine — just not while it's still starting.
  4. Put the phone back on its normal wifi / mobile data. Coil the cables. Count them.

5 · Troubleshooting

Work down the list. Nine times out of ten it's the first row.

ProblemWhat it meansWhat to do
Trouble 1 — Red dot 🔴The Pi is running but hasn't reached the board. Usually the Pi was powered before the board. Unplug the Pi, wait 10s, plug it back in (board already on), wait 60s. It retries on its own too — give it a moment first.
Trouble 2 — Page won't openPhone isn't on the board's wifi, or slipped to mobile data. Check the wifi says ledbox_C0270. Turn mobile data OFF. Reload 172.24.1.60:8890. Not listed at all → the board isn't powered.
Trouble 3 — Board stuck on an old scoreThe link dropped and came back. Tap any + then − — that forces a full repaint.
Trouble 4 — Board frozen / blank after restarting the PiThe board's control link wedged when the Pi dropped. Power-cycle the board (not just the Pi). Wait ~45s for it to come back, and the Pi reconnects on its own.
Trouble 5 — Name cut offKnown limit — long names overflow. Use a short name: WIE, VOL, GEN. Three or four letters always fit.
Trouble 6 — Blank page / missing buttonsStale page in the phone browser. Pull to refresh, or close the tab and scan SCAN 2 again.
Trouble 7 — Nothing worksStart from zero. Unplug BOTH. Count to ten. Board first, wait for its lights. Then the Pi, wait 60s. Reopen the page.
Trouble 8 — Dig deeperFor whoever has a laptop. ssh openvolley → systemctl status ledbox-bridge, journalctl -u ledbox-bridge -n 50. Restart: sudo systemctl restart ledbox-bridge. Board wifi dead → plug ethernet Pi↔board; it finds the board either way.
