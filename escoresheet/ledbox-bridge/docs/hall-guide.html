LedBox — Hall Guide

Set up the LED scoreboard and run a match. Follow the steps in order; don't skip the green checks.

The one rule

The scoreboard is self-contained — it makes its own wifi and runs everything itself. There's no separate box and no cable to plug in. Turn it on, join its wifi with your phone (scan the codes below — they're also shown on the board's own screen when it's idle), and score. Your phone must be on that wifi, not on mobile data.

SCAN 1
Join the scoreboard's wifi
ledbox_C0270 · wiedikon2026
Point your camera at the code → tap "Join". Then turn mobile data OFF.
SCAN 2
Open the controller
http://172.24.1.1:8890
Scan after you've joined the wifi. Bookmark the page when it opens.

2 · Setting up — in this order

  1. Stand or hang the board where players can see it. Plug it into mains.
    It boots itself — nothing to switch on separately.
    ✓ You should see: after ~45s it settles on the KSC Wiedikon crest, with a "Join WiFi" QR on the left and an "Open UI" QR on the right.
  2. Scan SCAN 1 (or the left code on the board) to join the wifi. Turn mobile data OFF.
    The wifi has no internet — that's normal. Phones jump back to mobile data unless you stop them.
  3. Scan SCAN 2 (or the right code on the board) to open the controller. Bookmark it.
    ✓ You should see: the control page — team panels, big + / − buttons, and (on a tablet) a live board mirror.
  4. Check the dot at the top-left.
    🟢 green = talking to the board, ready. 🔴 red = see Trouble 1.
  5. If a lock icon 🔒 shows top-right, tap it and enter the scorer PIN.
    Only the scorer needs it. Anyone else can open the page and watch, but can't change the score. No PIN set → no lock.
  6. Type both team names, pick colours. Turn the phone sideways; press ⛶ for full screen.
    Use SHORT names — WIE, VOL. Long names overflow on the board for now.

3 · Running the match — and your live test

Do these in order the first time; it doubles as a full check that everything works.

Do thisOn the board you should see
Tap + on a teamThe point appears and blinks in that team's colour; the serve indicator moves to that side.
Tap − (a correction)The point drops. It does not blink — only real points blink.
Tap a number (score, sets, T/O, sub)A little editor opens — type the exact value, press Set. For fixing a miscount fast. Typed values don't blink.
T/O + for a teamBoard switches to a timeout screen: "TO <team>", the score, and a 30s clock. At zero, the horn sounds. Press ✕ to end early — no horn. (Asks you to confirm first.)
T/O + again (to the limit)That timeout counter turns dark red — none left.
Sub +The sub counter blinks. One short of the limit it goes amber, at the limit dark red. (Confirms first.)
Reach 25 (2-point lead)The winning point blinks, board holds the final score, and the phone shows "SET ENDED · Start interval →". Tap it → the board shows the INTERVAL countdown. Tap Start next set →: score resets, ends swap, timeouts & subs reset.
⏱ Warm-upFull-screen WARM UP clock. Horn at zero.
🏐 VS / idleThe crest + team names — the pre-match / idle screen (with the QR codes).
⇄ SwapTeams change ends — names, colours, scores swap together.
History tabFinished matches, with per-set scores. Export CSV or JSON from there.
☀ Keep awakeHighlights when on; stops the phone/tablet sleeping. Stays on across a reload.
⚙ SettingsBlink, countdown lengths, horn, best-of, totals, club name, and Access → Scorer PIN. Saved on the board.

Keep in mind

Turn on ☀ Keep awake at the start so the phone doesn't sleep mid-match. Keep it on the ledbox_C0270 wifi — wander out of range and the page pauses; walk back and it reconnects. The score is safe: it lives on the board, not the phone, so you can lock the phone, swap phones, or hand over — nothing is lost. More than one phone can be connected at once; they all show the same score. Keep one person scoring (a second scorer double-counts).

4 · Packing up

  1. Settings → Power → ⏻ Shut down (confirm).
    Halts the board cleanly — this protects the memory card. Never just pull the plug while it's running.
    ✓ You should see: the phone shows "Now press the power button" and the panel goes dark within a few seconds.
  2. Unplug the board from mains.
    Only after the panel has gone dark. It starts itself next time — nothing to launch by hand.
  3. Put the phone back on its normal wifi / mobile data. Coil the cable.

5 · Troubleshooting

Work down the list. Nine times out of ten it's the first row.

ProblemWhat it meansWhat to do
Trouble 1 — Red dot 🔴The controller can't reach the board's display just yet. Right after power-on the display takes a few extra seconds — wait ~30s and it goes green on its own. Still red after a minute → power-cycle the board (off at the mains, wait 10s, on) and wait ~45s.
Trouble 2 — Page won't openPhone isn't on the board's wifi, or slipped to mobile data. Check the wifi says ledbox_C0270. Turn mobile data OFF. Reload 172.24.1.1:8890. Wifi not listed at all → the board isn't powered / still booting (wait for the crest).
Trouble 3 — Can't change the scoreThe scorer lock (PIN) is on. Tap the 🔒 top-right and enter the PIN. Watching works without it. Forgot the PIN → see Trouble 8.
Trouble 4 — Board stuck on an old scoreThe link dropped and came back. Tap any + then − — that forces a full repaint.
Trouble 5 — Name cut offKnown limit — long names overflow. Use a short name: WIE, VOL, GEN. Three or four letters always fit.
Trouble 6 — Blank page / missing buttonsStale page in the phone browser. Pull to refresh, or close the tab and scan SCAN 2 again.
Trouble 7 — Nothing worksStart from zero. Off at the mains, count to ten, on again. Wait ~45s for the crest, rejoin the wifi, reopen the page.
Trouble 8 — Dig deeperFor whoever has a laptop + access. Reach the board over the club network / Tailscale, then systemctl status ledbox-bridge, journalctl -u ledbox-bridge -n 50, restart sudo systemctl restart ledbox-bridge. The scorer PIN is at Settings → Access.
