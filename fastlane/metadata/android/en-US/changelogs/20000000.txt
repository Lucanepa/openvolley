OpenVolley 2.0.0, first release on F-Droid.
- Automatic backup of the open match after every scoring event; restore it under Options, Backup.
- Venue mode: referee and bench tablets list only the matches they can join; LED scoreboards see every published match, test matches too on a local server.
- Clearer connection status at a venue (Local only, Offline).
- Licence and credits (GPL-3.0-or-later) under Options, App version.
- No federation logo in the scoresheet PDF header on Android.
