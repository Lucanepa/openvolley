OpenVolley 2.0.0, first release on F-Droid.
- Portrait everywhere except the scoreboard (locks to landscape).
- Automatic backup of the open match after every scoring event; restore it under Options, Backup.
- Venue mode: referee and bench tablets list only the matches they can join; LED scoreboards see every published match.
- Clearer connection status at a venue (Local only, Offline).
- Licence and credits under Options, App version.
- No federation logo in the PDF header.
